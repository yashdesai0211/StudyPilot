STUDYPILOT · PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Created by Yash Desai

Privacy by default

StudyPilot works without an account. In this release, subjects, chapters, notes, quiz records, preferences and focus-session records are stored locally in the browser or installed web app on the device where they are entered.

What this release does not do

StudyPilot does not ask for an email address, phone number, password or account registration. It has no cloud database, external AI/API connection, advertising SDK or analytics tracker. The application does not intentionally send study records to a StudyPilot server.

Backups and deletion

You can export a backup, import a backup, or delete locally stored StudyPilot data from Settings. Exported files are your responsibility to protect. Clearing browser/app storage or changing devices may make records unavailable. Data cannot be restored without a backup.

Security limits

Local browser storage is not encrypted by this application. Anyone with access to an unlocked device and browser profile may be able to access records. Do not store passwords, highly sensitive information, or another person's private information.

Children and teenagers

This release avoids account creation and server-side collection of student profiles. Before adding online accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, public sharing or other collection of personal data, the product must undergo a fresh privacy and legal review with appropriate safeguards and any required consent processes.

Important

This is a plain-language draft for the current local-only release, not legal advice or a legal guarantee. Before public commercial distribution, obtain qualified review and update this notice to reflect the actual deployed product, hosting provider and applicable Indian law.
