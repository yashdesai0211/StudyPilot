# StudyPilot — Terms of Use (Draft)

**Last updated: 5 November 2026**  
**Product creator: Yash Desai**

StudyPilot is a study organisation and self-assessment tool. By using it, you agree to use it lawfully and responsibly.

## What the app does

StudyPilot lets users organise subjects and chapters, record learning status, view suggested revision dates, create their own quizzes, write notes and record focus sessions. Progress indicators are based only on information entered by the user. They are not a measure of intelligence, a guarantee of learning, or a prediction of examination marks.

## Your content

You are responsible for the subjects, notes, questions and other content you enter. Do not upload or share content you do not have permission to use, or content that violates another person's privacy or rights.

## Availability and data

This release stores data locally. It has no cloud sync or account recovery. Keep your own backups. The application is provided without a guarantee that it will be uninterrupted, error-free or compatible with every device/browser.

## Independent educational tool

StudyPilot is not affiliated with any school, examination board or government authority. Users should verify syllabus, examination and educational information with official sources.

## Responsible use

Do not use StudyPilot to harass, impersonate, cheat, distribute unlawful material or violate the rights of others. Do not rely on it as a substitute for official school or examination guidance.
