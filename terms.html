What StudyPilot does

StudyPilot is a study organisation and self-assessment tool. It lets users organise subjects and chapters, record learning status, view suggested revision dates, create their own quizzes, write notes and record focus sessions. Progress indicators are based only on information entered by the user. They are not a measure of intelligence, a guarantee of learning, or a prediction of examination marks.
